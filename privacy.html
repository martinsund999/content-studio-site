Privacy Policy

Content Studio. Effective 19 September 2026.

Who operates this tool

Content Studio is a private software tool operated by Martin Sund, Sweden ("the owner"). It is used exclusively by the owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What Google user data is accessed

Through the YouTube Data API v3 and the YouTube Analytics API, and only after the owner has granted consent through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow, the tool accesses the owner's own channel data: video uploads and their metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time, captions, thumbnails), playlists, comments on the owner's videos, ratings, and channel analytics such as views, impressions and watch time.

Requested scopes: youtube.force-ssl, youtube.upload and yt-analytics.readonly.

How the data is used

The data is used only to operate the owner's channel: to upload and schedule videos produced by the tool, to keep metadata, captions and playlists up to date, to reply to and moderate comments on the owner's own videos, and to read the channel's analytics in order to plan future videos. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner's channel.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and the channel data retrieved are stored on a server owned and controlled by the owner, located on the owner's private network in Sweden, protected by file permissions and network isolation. Data is retained only as long as it is needed to operate the channel and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party, except to Google itself through its APIs as required to perform the actions above. The tool uses OpenAI services to generate narration, illustrations and scripts; no Google user data is sent to those services.

Limited Use disclosure

Content Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens immediately.

YouTube Terms of Service

By using the YouTube API Services, this tool and its owner are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
